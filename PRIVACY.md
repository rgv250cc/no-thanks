# No Thanks privacy policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

No Thanks does not collect, sell or share your data. It has no account, analytics, ads or remote code, and it does not send anything to the developer. Filter lists are part of the extension, so it does not download them.

## What stays in your browser

- **Your settings and exceptions**, including the site and page addresses you add as exceptions, are kept in your browser's extension storage. If browser sync is on, your browser syncs them to your other devices through your browser account. That sync is run by your browser's provider under its own privacy policy.
- **Manual mute choices and "Allow once" sites** are remembered for the current browser session and then discarded.
- **Elements you hide** are kept in your browser's local extension storage, with the site they are on. They do not sync.

## What websites see

- **Global Privacy Control:** while this setting is on, your browser tells every site you visit not to sell or share your data, through a request header and a page property. You can turn it off.
- **Cookie banners:** while "Reject cookies" is on, No Thanks clicks "reject" on a site's cookie banner for you, so the site receives that choice.

## How it uses page addresses

No Thanks reads the address of your tabs on your device to decide which of your rules applies. It does not record your browsing history.

## Permissions

- **Content scripts and host access on all sites:** stop media from starting by itself, hide ads and clutter, stop page tricks and apply your rules, on any site you visit.
- **Declarative net request:** block ads, trackers and dangerous sites inside the browser. No Thanks does not see or record the requests.
- **Scripting:** run the page scripts that belong to the filter lists, on the sites your rules cover.
- **Unlimited storage:** keep the filter data for hiding elements on your device.
- **Tabs:** read tab addresses to apply your rules, and mute or unmute tabs.
- **Storage:** save your settings and exceptions.
- **Context menus:** add the right-click quick menu.

## Removing your data

Removing the extension deletes its local data. Synced data is managed through your browser account.
